# Lattice Health

> AI governance as a managed service for hospitals. Lattice Health monitors every clinical
> AI model running in a hospital across vendors, tapping the HL7 and DICOM signals those
> models already emit, and delivers a per-role cryptographically signed PDF report each
> morning covering drift (PSI), subgroup fairness disparity, safety posture, latency,
> silent vendor model-update detection, and aggregated radiologist feedback. Reports are
> signed with the institution's own key and verify offline with standard open-source
> tooling, with no Lattice account or infrastructure required. Y Combinator Spring 2026,
> San Francisco.

## Status of the developer surface

## What the platform does

- Ingest: taps existing HL7 and DICOM feeds from deployed vendor AI models, no rip-and-replace.
- Measure: continuous population stability index (PSI) drift, subgroup fairness disparity,
  latency p95 against target, and statistical silent-update detection on confidence distributions.
- Report: per-role signed PDFs (IT Director, CMIO/CIO, Compliance Officer) delivered by email;
  a missed day is an SLA event.
- Prove: chain-of-custody evidence packets, transparency-log anchored, for decisions later
  under regulatory or legal scrutiny.
- Feedback: one-click radiologist thumbs-up/thumbs-down on AI results, aggregated at model
  level only so individual labor data stays private.

## Standards alignment (vendor claims, not third-party certifications)

- IHE-aligned: UPS-RS work-list status, AI-Results, ERA classification
- DICOM and HL7 signal ingest
- HIPAA-safe egress: PHI redactor, strict-mode default, batch-blocking failure
- HHS Section 1557 subgroup fairness disparity reporting
- FDA Predetermined Change Control Plan (PCCP) envelope status with breach detection
- ONC HTI-1, ACR ARCH-AI, ACR Assess-AI continuous posture
- Air-gap friendly: no external API required for the core workflow
